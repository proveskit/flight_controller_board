# pcb-flight-review final list (chair merge)

Chair merge of the PCB and QA lists: 12 items from the same 16 verified findings, 9 of them with identical severity/disposition from both principals. Four are fix-this-rev on the PM's own criterion (bench mode fails or hardware is stressed), all confined to the bench strip and the owner-ruled attachment framework, none touching frozen FC-copy copper: (1) Mode 2 feeds the FC from BQ25886 BAT instead of SYS and cannot keep the FC up; (2) all bench battery current enters the FC copy through the J16.8 debug stub and the heritage 0.4 mm inner trace (0.2-0.36 ohm) instead of the J14 attachment the brief specified; (3) the R5460 replica's V-/VSS sense loop contains 64-75 mOhm of 0.25-0.30 mm copper so excess-discharge trips at 2.0-2.5 A, on top of the 2 A bench limit; (4) the BQ25886 SYS hot loop runs through two 0.2 mm vias and In2 with no GND via at any charger cap and no EP vias, done in the same pass as (1). Three disagreements decided: the U200 VREG cell goes monitor-in-test with a next-rev copy of the flown U18/L3 cell (QA position; the PM criterion is failure or damage, neither is shown, the rotate-and-swap is withdrawn by both principals, and reopening the EMU_FANOUT area carries its own regression risk); the BenchPower track_width DRC rule and net labels go in this rev (PCB position; it is the acceptance check for items 2-4 and costs nothing); the VSOLAR bench hard maximum drops from 24 V to 18 V (PCB position; heritage C40 on V_SOLAR_SENSE is a 10 V X5R, the stated nominal window already tops out at 18 V, and the change is text only). Rank 2/3 order follows the PCB principal: the J16.8 path affects every test in every mode and loads heritage copper, the replica trip only manifests at the top of the window. Remaining items are next-rev with this-rev procedure mitigations, one accepted by owner ruling R9, and the QSPI length topic is closed with no action at CLKDIV >= 2. Nothing in the packet can damage the FC copy at bench currents of 1 A or less.

## 1. [major / fix-this-rev / small] Mode 2 (USB charger) feeds the FC from BQ25886 BAT, not SYS: charge termination, recharge deglitch and safety timer cut FC power

**Location.** battery_protection_replica.kicad_sch: U511.13/14 BAT (CHG_BAT) -> JP510.1 -> JP510.2 Dir_Chrg_In (JP510 description: 'charger BAT -> Dir_Chrg_In'). CHG_SYS = U511.15/16 + C513/C514/C517/C518 + TP511 (265.5,144.8), no load. R515 5.62k -> ICHG 1.475 A, ITERM/IPRECHG ~147 mA. PCB: JP510 (257.3,148.33), U511 (277.0,152.2), SYS via (279.663,152.2). Packet ids 3 + 17.

**Rationale.** Both principals: major, fix-this-rev, same physics. D11 forbids a pack in Mode 2, so BAT, a charge-controlled port, is the FC's only source. FC idle current (100 mA on 3.3 V reflects to 50-80 mA at 8.4 V) is below ITERM, so after the 250 ms tTERM_DGL the BATFET opens (SLUSD88A 8.3.7.3), the BAT/FC input capacitance collapses in ms, the 250 ms recharge deglitch then restarts it: a brownout/reboot loop, with the FC as the victim of the 'expected STAT blink'. Above ITERM the 12 h (24 h in DPM) safety timer suspends BAT. Start-up is limited to 100 mA below 4.4 V and 147 mA below 6.0 V. SYS is the datasheet's no-battery instant-on output (8.3.6.1, regulated >= 6.2 V, VBAT+50 mV after termination), and it is unloaded. Deterministic, one of three documented bench modes fails, no damage path, Mode 1 unaffected, debug_board_v1 is not heritage.

**Action.** Schematic: move JP510 pin 1 from CHG_BAT to CHG_SYS; leave CHG_BAT with C515 and TP512 only. PCB: delete the CHG_BAT In1 0.25 mm run (272.25,146.13)->JP510.1; route JP510.1 from the CHG_SYS copper at TP511 (265.5,144.8) on F.Cu at >= 1.0 mm (~8.4 mm, BenchPower). Keep the rule 'JP510 never with J500 PSU'. Rewrite the Mode 2 sheet note: FC fed from SYS, STAT blink is BAT-cap cycling. If boards are ordered first: JP510 open, 22 AWG wire TP511 -> TP500 (190.0,163.5).

**Verification.** Mode 2 bring-up (USB only, no pack, JP500 open, JP510 fitted): log Dir_Chrg_In at >= 1 kS/s with the FC idle for 30 min and under a 0.5 A load step; pass = no excursion below 6.0 V, no FC reset, 8.2-8.5 V. Then a > 13 h soak with the FC above 200 mA to clear the safety-timer case. As built, the idle test shows reboot cycling within seconds, so the ATP step is diagnostic either way.

## 2. [major / fix-this-rev / medium] All bench battery current enters the FC copy through J16.8 DEBUG and the heritage 0.4 mm inner debug-charge trace (0.2-0.36 ohm), not the J14 attachment the brief specified

**Location.** Net Dir_Chrg_In: J500.1 (202.92,165.2) / JP510.2 -> ~140 mm of new 1.0 mm track through five single 0.46/0.20 vias (246.0,157.66), (246.0,131.56), (241.1,126.26), (243.0,90.46), (234.9,65.66) -> F.Cu stub to J16.8 (224.5,65.0) -> heritage In2 0.4 mm x=226.6 from y=68.4 to 125.7 (57.3 mm) -> via (220.48,130.28) -> 0.4 mm B.Cu -> J14.1 (207.0,131.2) -> R109/U16. No new copper on J14 pins 1/3/5/7/9. Packet id 15.

**Rationale.** Both principals: major, fix-this-rev. Ranked 2 (PCB order) over the replica trip because it affects every Mode-1 and Mode-2 test and is the only bench-current path into the FC copy. Brief L11 and floorplan_v2 put the battery attachment at J14; routing took the shortest legal stub to J16.8. The heritage J16.8 -> J14.1 link (0.10-0.21 ohm) never carried amps on the flown board (debug-board ILIM error gave ~3 mA). Total 0.20-0.36 ohm: 0.4-0.7 V drop and 0.4-0.85 W in frozen FC-copy copper at the 2 A bench limit, 0.2-0.36 V at 1 A. Thermal stress is tens of degrees on a 0.4 mm 0.5 oz inner trace, not destructive ('damage' framing softened by both principals), but it changes the behaviour of heritage copper (the PM's stated exception) and is a permanent fidelity error: the replica senses at J500 while the FC and INA219 U16 see hundreds of mV less, so brown-out and VBATT-telemetry tests near the 4.9 V floor do not match a pack on J14. Five single 0.2 mm vias in series also miss brief L5 (two 0.8/0.4 per layer change). Attempt-1 reached J14.2 on F.Cu, so a compliant stub exists.

**Action.** Add an L11-compliant Dir_Chrg_In stub to J14 odd pins 1/3/5/7/9 (THT at (207.0,131.2), rot 90, 2.0 mm pitch, any layer) at >= 1.0 mm (1.5 mm preferred) with paired 0.8/0.4 vias at each layer change, landing on two or more of the odd pins; keep the J16.8 stub as a sense-only tap or remove it. Without a respin: DF11-12 mating pigtail from the J14 power pins to J500/JP510, cap sustained bench current at 1 A otherwise, and print the measured 0.2-0.36 ohm drop in the bring-up document.

**Verification.** 4-wire resistance J500.1 -> R109.1 at 1.0 A: target < 50 mOhm (as built 0.20-0.36 ohm). IR camera along x~226.6 mm, y 68-126 mm during a 2 A pulse for 10 min: no spot > 20 C above ambient. DRC with the item-6 width rule shows the J14 stub at >= 1.0 mm.

## 3. [major / fix-this-rev / small] Protection-replica current path (0.25-0.30 mm copper, 0.5 oz In1 neck) sits inside the R5460 V-/VSS sense loop: excess-discharge trips at ~2.0-2.5 A instead of ~11 A, coincident with the 2 A bench limit

**Location.** battery_protection_replica / PCB bench strip: Net-(Q500-D-Pad5) Q500 pads 5-8 (195.075,153.5-157.4) -> 0.25 mm F.Cu -> via 0.46/0.20 (196.95,152.94) -> In1 0.25 mm 6.88 mm -> via (202.23,156.82) -> Q501 pads 5-8. VBAT_BENCH_N J500.3 (213.08,165.2) -> In2 1.5/1.0 mm -> single via (194.963,161.288) -> R505 -> F.Cu 0.3 mm 3.22 mm -> via (191.286,158.546) -> B.Cu -> via (191.436,155.846) -> Q500 S. Sense: U500.6 VSS taps VBAT_BENCH_N by a separate no-load route; U500.3 V- via R502 taps B- at (203.856,148.403). Packet ids 7 + 12 + replica part of 16.

**Rationale.** Both principals: major, fix-this-rev. QA reproduced the drain-link geometry (38.9 / 48.9 mOhm pad-to-pad, 31 mOhm in the In1 neck); the id 12 verifier traced the full loop: drain 37 + VBAT_BENCH_N 22-32 + B- to the R502 tap 6 = 64-75 mOhm against 16-20 mOhm of FET Rds(on). VDET3 0.200 +/- 0.015 V then corresponds to 2.0-2.5 A nominal, ~1.96 A at VDET3 min, lower as the copper warms (the id 7 verifier's 3.5 A counted the drain link only). At the sheet's 2 A PSU limit the sensed drop is 0.17-0.19 V, inside the trip band, so a heater/deploy test at the top of the window latches DOUT (release only on load removal) and browns out the FC: the copper, not the pack model, decides, and the replica misrepresents battery_pack_v2, its only purpose. The In1 neck dissipates ~0.13 W at 2 A (IPC-2221 internal 0.25 mm/0.5 oz ~0.24 A at 10 C rise). route_report §7's claim that sub-1.0 mm BenchPower copper is only L11 stubs is false here. Bench-strip copper only; no FC-copy damage.

**Action.** Rotate/mirror Q501 so its drain pads 5-8 face Q500 pads 5-8 and tie the rows with an F.Cu pour >= 2 mm wide (< 5 mOhm), no inner-layer segment; delete the In1 0.25 mm neck and vias (196.95,152.94)/(202.23,156.82). Widen VBAT_BENCH_N from J500.3 to the Q500 source pads to >= 1.0 mm on F/B.Cu and pair every via on that path with 0.8/0.4 vias. Local label on the common-drain net, add to BenchPower. Target < 10 mOhm of copper inside the V-/VSS loop (trip > 7 A); correct the sheet note 'about 11 A' to the computed value. Without a respin: tinned braid Q500 pads 5-8 to Q501 pads 5-8, wire J500.3 to the Q500 source pads, PSU limit 1.5 A, note that DOUT trips at ~2 A.

**Verification.** 4-wire Q500 pad 8 to Q501 pad 5 <= 5 mOhm (as built 39-49 mOhm). V(U500.6) - V(U500.3) at 1.0 A resistive load on Dir_Chrg_In ~20-25 mV (as built ~90 mV); V(J500.3) - V(R502 tap) at 2.0 A <= 60 mV (as built 170-190 mV). ATP: 2.0 A load for 60 s cold and after 10 min at 1.5 A: TP503/TP504 gate drive stays high, no DOUT latch.

## 4. [major / fix-this-rev / medium] BQ25886 SYS hot loop violates layout priority 1: CSYS reached through two 0.2 mm vias and In2, GND return 7.6-19 mm, no EP vias, no GND via within 3 mm of any charger cap

**Location.** battery_protection_replica / PCB: U511 (277.0,152.2) SYS pins 15/16 (278.96,151.95/152.45) -> 0.25 mm F.Cu -> via 0.46/0.20 (279.663,152.2) -> In2 ~5.7 mm -> via (281.462,154.95) -> C513.1 (280.845,156.55); C513.2 GND -> 7.61 mm F.Cu to via (288.4,151.55) or 19.2 mm to pins 19/20; EP pad 25 (2.7x2.7) 0 vias; nearest GND via to C511/C513/C514/C515/C517/C518 GND pads 4.2-6.1 mm; SW ~2.1 mm 0.25 mm; PMID 0.25 mm F.Cu + 7.15 mm In2 + 2 vias; CHG_BAT In1 0.25 mm 14.4 mm under U511. Packet id 11 + charger parts of 8 and 16.

**Rationale.** Both principals: major, fix-this-rev, coupled to item 1. The boost hot loop (HS FET -> SYS -> CSYS -> PGND -> LS FET) carries ~2 A peaks with ns edges through several nH of extra loop inductance against 1-2 nH for the TI layout (SLUSD88A 11.1 item 1), so SW overshoot above an 8.4-8.6 V SYS can plausibly approach the 13 V SW absolute maximum (7.1 footnote). Plausible, not measured: hardware damage to U511 only. JP510 is open by default and a failed U511 cannot put more than ~5 V on Dir_Chrg_In, so the FC copy is not exposed. EP vias (11.1 item 6) are a real but mild miss (0.6-0.7 W, TREG 120 C throttles); the PMID In2 run and 0.25 mm SW/CHG_BAT runs are resistive losses outside the hot loop at the real 0.8-1.1 A charge current. After item 1, SYS also feeds the FC, so CSYS quality matters more, and CHG_BAT carries no load. Item 1 reopens this exact area, so the incremental cost is modest; QA's condition is adopted: if the owner takes the bench-wire route for item 1 instead of a respin, this drops to monitor-in-test with the scope gate below.

**Action.** In the same pass as item 1: place C513 (22 uF) plus a 0.1-1 uF 25 V 0402 directly across SYS pins 15/16 and PGND 19/20 on F.Cu (swap with C512 BTST if space is needed); 3x3 array of 0.3 mm vias in the EP to In1/B.Cu; a GND via (2x 0.46/0.20 or 1x 0.8/0.4) at every 22 uF cap GND pad (C510, C511, C513, C514, C515, C517, C518); SW >= 0.8 mm; PMID to L510.2 on F.Cu >= 0.8 mm (rotate L510 so pad 2 faces C511); delete the CHG_BAT In1 run under U511. Fallback without respin: scope SW with a ground-spring probe at 5 V / 1.48 A input and 1 A BAT load; if peaks exceed 11 V, raise R514 to derate ILIM to ~1 A and record it.

**Verification.** Design: GND via within 1 mm of every charger cap GND pad, >= 6 vias in the EP, CSYS loop pins-to-cap-to-pins under 5 mm on F.Cu. Bench: SW (pin 17) peak < 11 V at ILIM 1.48 A / VBUS 5.25 V with 1 A load; SYS ripple at C513 < 100 mVpp; U511 case < 60-80 C after 30 min at ILIM. ATP would otherwise see this only as a dead charger, so the scope check is the gate.

## 5. [major / monitor-in-test / trivial] U200 core-regulator cell is not the mandated RP2350 reference/flown layout (LX on In2 through a POFV via-in-pad, three GND islands, COUT return under L200, CFILT sharing COUT's via); L200 polarity is mirror-consistent as built and the proposed rotate-and-swap is withdrawn

**Location.** emulator_mcu: U200 (273.145,94.855) pins 47-50 at y=91.418; L200 (279.4,90.75) rot 0, pad 1 (dot, 278.7) = EMU_VREG_LX, pad 2 (280.1) = 1V1_EMU; C217 COUT (278.8,92.4) rot 180; C213 CIN (276.62,90.15); C215 CFILT (278.8,93.91). EMU_VREG_LX: pin 48 -> F.Cu 0.127 mm 0.87 mm -> via 0.40/0.20 (275.145,90.55) -> In2 0.25 mm 3.1 mm inside the 3V3_EMU pour -> POFV via (278.403,90.408) in L200.1. COUT GND return 0.127 mm x 3.4 mm under the L200 body to via (279.66,89.575). VREG_AVDD In2 ~1.25 mm from LX. R703/R704 at (272.845,89.1)/(274.645,89.1) north of the pins. Flown reference: U18 (206.08,85.42,-45), L3 (212.9,81.7) pad 1 = +1V1, pad 2 = VREG_LX, F.Cu only, one GND island. Packet ids 10 + 13 + refuted L200 entry.

**Rationale.** Both principals agree on the facts and both withdraw the rotate-and-swap: against RP2350 DS 6.3.8.3 / Fig. 28 and Raspberry Pi's stated mechanism (leakage-field direction set by winding and current flow coupling into the LX-L-COUT loop), the FlatSat L200/C217 pair is the left-right mirror of the reference pair with the same-chirality part (flown loop CCW, current non-dot -> dot; emulator loop CW, current dot -> non-dot), so the as-built dot-on-LX orientation has the same coupling sign and the swap would most likely create the wrong-way case. Disposition disagreement decided for QA (monitor-in-test, next-rev re-layout) over PCB (fix-this-rev, copy the flown cell now): the PM's criterion for fix-this-rev is bench failure or hardware damage; the exposure here is degraded 1V1_EMU regulation/noise under load transients, which RPi says non-reference layouts generally survive ('could work', designer's risk), and no measurement shows failure. The PCB remedy moves R703/R704/C212 and re-routes inside the EMU_FANOUT rule area closed under owner rulings F13/F16, a medium rework in the densest region of the board with its own regression risk, while the symmetric L200 pads make the only contested physics a two-minute bench A/B. Severity stays major because, if it manifests, every bench test depends on the emulator. The id 13 verifier confirmed the fills are current.

**Action.** Do NOT apply the pin-swap-and-rotate this rev. Bring-up gate: measure 1V1_EMU at TP202 (280.0,114.5) with a short-ground 500 MHz probe at idle and under a 0 -> 150 mA core load step (both cores idle -> SDK busy loop at 150 MHz with QSPI XIP reads), compared with the FC copy's +1V1 under the same step. If ripple, sub-harmonic bursting or overshoot is worse than the FC's, rotate L200 180 deg (pads symmetric, routing unchanged), re-measure, keep the better orientation and record it. Next rev: copy the flown U18/L3/C65/C66/C67 cell verbatim (same handedness and pin mapping: pad 1 dot = 1V1, pad 2 = LX; CIN ~1.2 mm, COUT ~2.1 mm, L ~3.75 mm from the pins, centred on pin 48; LX on F.Cu with no via; one F.Cu GND island for CIN/COUT/PGND with 2 adjacent vias; C215 on its own via; no copper under L; VREG_AVDD away from LX), moving R703/R704/C212 ~2 mm west; this removes one of the 7 POFV vias (L200.1). Add a U200-cell-vs-U18 parity check to heritage.py. Optional: if the owner reopens routing broadly for items 1-4, the copy may ride that pass at the owner's call.

**Verification.** TP202 1V1_EMU: ripple < 50 mVpp idle, no sub-harmonic bursting, load-step excursion < 100 mV, within 20 % of / no worse than FC +1V1 in the same test; repeat after a 180 deg L200 rotation. Next rev: pad-net dump shows L200 pad 1 = 1V1_EMU, EMU_VREG_LX has 0 vias, cell geometry diff against U18 shows identical relative placement.

## 6. [minor / fix-this-rev / small] Bench power-net width is not DRC-enforced and KiCad-auto-named power nodes have no netclass (root cause of items 2-4); residual 0.19-0.30 mm runs on injection, VBUS_CHG, CHG_DVIN and the VBUSP/INHIB_2/B- stubs

Next rev (or now if the rule makes it cheap): D40x-K/A at >= 1.0 mm on outer layers, VBUS_CHG and the CHG_DVIN neck at >= 0.8-1.0 mm, widen the VBUSP/INHIB_2/B- stubs as far as the heritage pads allow. **Verification.** Run kicad-cli pcb drc with the new rule on the as-built board first: it must flag Net-(Q500-D-Pad5), CHG_BAT In1 and VBAT_BENCH_N 0.3 mm (proves the check), then report 0 track_width items outside the band after the items 2-4 re-routes. Next rev: thermal check of the D400-K/D401-K runs at 1.85 A for 5 min < 30 C rise.

## 7. [minor / fix-this-rev / trivial] VSOLAR injection has no on-board over-voltage element ahead of FC-copy INA219 U8 (accepted per owner ruling R9); lower the bench hard maximum from 24 V to 18 V because heritage C40 (LT3652 VIN bypass) is a 10 V X5R

**Location.** solar_power_injection.kicad_sch: J400/J401 -> F400/F401 (2920L 1.85 A PTC) -> D400/D401 Schottky -> JP400/JP401 -> VSOLAR -> U8.1 IN+, R108 2 mOhm -> IC6 LT3652 VIN; sheet note 'Bench PSU setting: 12 V to 18 V nominal, HARD MAXIMUM 24 V (brief section 6.3; F400/F401 are 33 V Vmax ...)'. Silk reads only 'VSOLAR A' (253.05,152.43) / 'VSOLAR B' (226.2,162.82). Packet id 18 + its verifier's C40 side note.

**Rationale.** Merges PCB items 8 and 10 and QA items 7 and 13. ## 8. [minor / next-rev / small] F1-F4 face-power sense taps land on non-FT RP2350 ADC pins (GPIO26-29): abs-max violation and back-feed into 3V3_EMU when the emulator USB is unplugged while faces are on

**Location.** emulator_mcu / solar_emulation: EMU_F1..F4_SENSE = R314/R324/R334/R344 (4.7k) from F1..F4_PWR (AP22653 U21/U22/U24/U23 outputs) to U200 pins 40-43 (GPIO26_ADC0..GPIO29_ADC3). 3V3_EMU sourced only by U202 from J701. F5 (GPIO24) and FC3V3 (GPIO22) senses are on FT pins. Packet id 5.

**Rationale.** Both principals: minor, next-rev. RP2350 DS Table 1674 lists GPIO26-29 as 'Digital IO / Analogue', not FT, so VPIN <= IOVDD + 0.5 V (Table 1680) applies; with faces 1-4 on and J701 absent the four pins sit at ~3.3 V through 4.7k with IOVDD near 0, injecting up to ~0.55 mA each (~2.2 mA total) and partially powering U200/U201/D201 with VREG_VIN below its 2.7 V minimum.
