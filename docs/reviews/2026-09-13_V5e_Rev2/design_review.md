# FC V5e Rev2 Design Review (2026-09-13)

Board chair merge of the PCB and QA principal lists (both derived from the same verified packet). The two lists agree on substance; the only disagreements were dispositions on JP6 (fix-before-flight vs accept), the LT3652 MPPT setpoint (next-rev vs accept), the ordering within the Rev3 decoupling batch, and whether the Rev2-delta confirmation is no-action or monitor-in-test. Decisions are stated per item. Chair spot-checks before ruling: C10 is at (210.625 74) at .kicad_pcb line 162092; U5 B.CrtYd rect is (start -2.82 3.97)(end 2.74 -3.86); the PCB principal's scratch DRCs show move-C10-only = 35 clearance + 2 courtyards_overlap (new C10/U5), move + courtyard trim = 35 clearance + 1 courtyards_overlap (inherited SW2/TP2), confirming the courtyard root cause and the two-part fix; FC_V5e_Production_Rev2/jlcpcb/production_files contains only BOM/CPL/GERBER-FC_V5e_Production_Rev1.* (no Rev2 outputs exist yet), confirming the QA regenerate-fab-files requirement. Verdict: Rev2 is orderable after one trivial layout correction (item 1) and one schematic-only hygiene edit (item 3), followed by refill/DRC and fresh Rev2 production files. Item 2 (JP6 watchdog bridge) is a mandatory pre-flight build/ATP action, not a design change. Everything else is inherited from V5d (flight) and/or Rev1 (bench + environmental), confirmed at minor/info, and batched for Rev3; the board explicitly declines to fold the cheap decoupling additions into Rev2 at order time because the C10 misregistration shows incidental layout edits on this board carry real regression risk and the current configuration is validated. Rev2 test scope must add PSRAM/QSPI-at-temperature with concurrent radio TX, since the PSRAM subsystem flew on V5d but was not on the Rev1 environmental article. Three packet findings were refuted and not carried (USB-C Rd, PSRAM 'no decoupling', LIS2MDL C1).

## 1. [minor / fix-this-rev / trivial] C10 (PSRAM bypass) dragged 0.125 mm off its V5d-heritage copper in commit 066aeb7 to dodge the new U5 courtyard; 4 new clearance errors incl. 0.0892 mm +3V3-to-GND from a stale B.Cu GND fill in the committed file (Rev2-only regression)

**Where:** FC_V5e_Production_Rev2.kicad_pcb line 162092: C10 C_0402_1005Metric B.Cu `(at 210.625 74)` [heritage position (210.500, 74.000)]; pad1 +3V3 (210.145,74.000), pad2 GND (211.105,74.000). Copper drawn for x=210.500: +3V3 stub end (210.020,74.000), GND stub start (210.980,74.000), GND via 0.6/0.3 at (210.2896,73.2032), QSPI_SCLK diagonal (209.8204,71.8204)->(211.7728,73.7728). Stale B.Cu GND zone uuid 8de2b9ba (priority 4). U5 footprint uuid b81480bf at (206.9,76.1,180), B.CrtYd fp_rect local (start -2.82 3.97)(end 2.74 -3.86) at line ~168493. Rev2 production folder jlcpcb/production_files holds only *-FC_V5e_Production_Rev1.* outputs.

**Why:** Both principals rank this first with identical evidence (packet ids 13 + 17, all four gaps reproduced to 4 decimals: 0.0892 / 0.1814 / 0.1868 / 0.1975 mm vs 0.200 mm netclass; at x=210.500 the same gaps are 0.2005 / 0.2699 / 0.2001 / 0.2858 mm). Chair concurs with minor severity: the 0.0892 mm figure is a stale fill (refill -> 0.2005), the other three are above the 0.127 mm board minimum and ~2x JLCPCB's floor, so physical short risk is low; but the committed file carries the stale fill and `kicad-cli pcb export gerbers` without --check-zones emits it verbatim, and this is the only Rev2-new actionable defect on the primary 3V3 rail's outer layer. Chair adopts the PCB principal's root cause (C10's 0402 courtyard at x=210.500 overlaps U5's hand-drawn courtyard by 0.13 mm, which is why C10 was nudged) and the two-part fix, verified in the scratch copy: move-only yields 35 clearance + 2 courtyards_overlap (new C10/U5), move + courtyard trim yields 35 clearance + 1 courtyards_overlap (the inherited SW2/TP2) = exact Rev1 baseline. Chair also adopts the QA principal's requirement to regenerate production files: the Rev2 folder currently contains only Rev1 BOM/CPL/gerbers, which do not include U5/C10 at all.

**Action:** (1) Move C10 to (210.500, 74.000); do not redraw the stubs or touch the GND via/track. (2) Trim U5's B.CrtYd fp_rect start-x from -2.82 to -2.65 (local; global x-max 209.72 -> 209.55) so it clears C10's courtyard -- the courtyard is a drawn rectangle, not a fab feature, and the C10/U5 physical spacing is V5d flight geometry. (3) Fill All Zones and save so the committed .kicad_pcb no longer carries the stale GND fill. (4) Re-run DRC: expect clearance 39 -> 35 (all inherited intra-footprint pad-pitch entries at U7/U15/U8/U16), courtyards_overlap 1 (SW2/TP2), zero items within x 208.5-212.5 / y 71-76. (5) Generate Rev2 production files (gerbers, BOM, CPL) from the GUI or with `kicad-cli pcb export gerbers --check-zones`; do not upload the Rev1 files in jlcpcb/production_files for Rev2. (6) Commit the refilled board and the Rev2 outputs.

**Verify:** `kicad-cli pcb drc --refill-zones --severity-all` and the same without --refill-zones must agree (no C10 entries either way); parse the exported B.Cu gerber and confirm GND-region-to-C10-pad1 gap >= 0.20 mm. ATP: +3V3-to-GND resistance check before first power-up on every Rev2 unit (a bridged gap presents as a dead short; pre-fab gerber check is the real gate).

## 2. [minor / fix-before-flight / trivial] Watchdog power-cycle path to the 3V3 regulator runs through open-by-default solder jumper JP6 (no paste aperture); flight state (JP6 bridged, J4 shunt off) not captured in any assembly note or ATP step, no test point on the JP6 nets

**Where:** watchdog.kicad_sch: U1 pin 13 (TLV1704 OUT4, open-collector, no pull-up) -> Net-(JP6-A) -> JP6 -> ~{3v3_RESET} -> U10 pin 7 (TPS54226 EN), R124 10k pull-up to VBUSP. PCB F.Cu: JP6 Jumper:SolderJumper-2_P1.3mm_Open_RoundedPad1.0x1.5mm at (171.1, 133.8), 0.3 mm gap, no F.Paste aperture. J4 PinHeader_1x02 on WDT_DISABLE/GND (shunt disables WDT). TP12 = WDT_DISABLE, TP13 = Net-(U1D-+); neither straddles JP6. BOM: U1 MPN TLV1704AQPWRQ1.

**Why:** Merges packet ids 11 + 19. Both principals agree on facts: as reflowed by JLCPCB the jumper is open, with it open the TLV1704 output floats and R124 holds EN high, and there is no other on-board path from the watchdog to any reset (FC_RESET has no U1 node; +3V3 comes straight off U10.1). Identical footprint on identical nets in V5d (flight) and Rev1, annotated 'WDT enabled when soldered', so a deliberate build option and not a Rev2 change. Disposition disagreement: PCB said fix-before-flight, QA said accept-with-rationale. Chair rules fix-before-flight: there is a concrete mandatory action (bridge JP6, remove J4 shunt, add ATP line) that must be executed on every flight unit, and 'accept' would misrepresent it as needing nothing; the design itself is accepted as-is. Consequence of a missed bridge is loss of the last-resort hang/SEL recovery for the whole +3V3 domain, which is why it outranks every other inherited item. Chair carries the QA side-note: the sheet annotation names TLV1704AMPWPSEP as the radiation-tolerant variant and TLV1704AQPWRQ1 as 'for non-flight C3 boards'; the BOM carries AQPWRQ1 in V5d, Rev1 and Rev2 -- heritage, not a finding, but the build lot should confirm the intended variant.

**Action:** Add to the Rev2 assembly drawing / fab notes: 'JP6 bridged for flight, open for bench only; J4 unpopulated / shunt removed on flight units'. Add ATP pass/fail lines: (a) continuity U1.13 to U10.7 (< 1 ohm when bridged) measured at the pins or JP6 pads, since TP12/TP13 do not cover these nets; (b) functional: hold WDT_WDI (RP2350 GPIO23) static and confirm +3V3 drops and returns within ~36 s, then confirm normal kicking keeps the rail up for > 2 minutes; (c) photograph JP6 in the build record. Confirm U1 variant (AQPWRQ1 vs AMPWPSEP) is the intended one for this lot. Optional Rev3 hardening: test point on ~{3v3_RESET} and/or a fitted 0R 0402 (DNP for bench) in place of the solder jumper -- do NOT change this on Rev2, it departs from the flown configuration without re-validation.

**Verify:** The ATP functional test catches both an unbridged JP6 and a J4 shunt left on; the pin-level continuity check catches JP6 alone. The functional test also exercises the full TLV1704 timing chain (item 7).

## 3. [minor / fix-this-rev / trivial] ~{5V_RESET} is an orphan single-node global label on MCP23017 GPB6 (reads as a reset that is not wired); RF_VCC (E22 1 W radio) has no on-board power-cycle path

**Where:** Root/EPS schematic: net ~{5V_RESET} = [U9 pin 7 GPB6] only (ERC single_global_label + isolated_pin_label). U12 LT8610AB pin 4 EN/UV tied to VBUSP with VIN pins 5/6; RF_VCC (~4.96 V, R63 1M / R23 243k) feeds U13 E22-400M30S pins 9/10 with no load switch. U13 NRST is driven by RP2350 GPIO6 (RF1_RST: R97, U13.15, U18.9).

**Why:** Packet id 20, confirmed with corrections by both principals (NRST is GPIO-driven; U12 pin 14 is BIAS not the output; 'unrecoverable on orbit' is not established because VBUSP arrives off-board via J20/J30 RBF so a spacecraft-level bus cut may exist). Label is absent from V5d and is a V5e-era artifact present in Rev1. Both principals agree: fix-this-rev, schematic-only, zero copper change, so it is safe to do before ordering and prevents flight software being written against a non-existent reset. Ranked 3 by the chair because it is the only other pre-order action; the architectural point (RF rail not independently power-cyclable) is an inherited scope decision for the FMEA, not a Rev2 change.

**Action:** Before order: delete the ~{5V_RESET} global label on U9 pin 7 and place a no-connect flag (or rename GPB6_SPARE); run Update PCB from Schematic and confirm no copper diff (only the U9 pad 7 net name changes). Documentation: FMEA entry stating RF_VCC/E22 cannot be power-cycled from the FC board, that radio SEL/hang recovery depends on GPIO6 NRST plus spacecraft-level VBUSP removal via the EPS/RBF path, and confirm with the EPS team that such a cycle exists. Rev3 option if radio SEL recovery is required: drive U12 EN/UV from a GPIO through a divider preserving UVLO, or add a current-limited load switch on RF_VCC.

**Verify:** ERC after the edit: single_global_label 2 -> 1, isolated_pin_label 1 -> 0; netlist diff shows only the U9 pin 7 net rename; grep flight firmware for any reference to 5V_RESET / GPB6 as a reset. FMEA sign-off for the RF_VCC recovery path.

## 4. [minor / next-rev / small] LT3652 solar input-regulation (MPPT) setpoint 8.45 V is below the charger's minimum operating VIN for the 8.38 V float, so peak-power tracking never engages; not fixable without array Vmp/Voc data

**Where:** eps_side.kicad_sch: IC6 LT3652IMSE pin 2 VIN_REG; R65 100k + R66 100k (V_SOLAR_SENSE -> VIN_REG), R69 94k (VIN_REG -> GND); float divider R71 634k / R74 412k; R75 0.1 ohm (ICHG max 1 A).

**Why:** Packet id 4, confirmed numerically by both: VIN_REG setpoint 2.7 x 294/94 = 8.445 V; VBAT(FLT) = 3.3 x (1 + 634/412) = 8.378 V; LT3652 datasheet (3652fd, VIN pin / Note 4) minimum operating VIN = VBAT(FLT) + 0.75 = 9.13 V, start-up = VBAT(FLT) + 3.3 = 11.68 V; at VIN = 9.13 V the VIN_REG pin is 2.92 V > 2.70 V threshold so foldback cannot occur inside the valid window and the part behaves as if VIN_REG were tied to VIN (datasheet-sanctioned). Charging works; harvest efficiency is lost. Divider is bit-identical in V5d (flown) and Rev1. Disposition disagreement: PCB next-rev, QA accept-with-rationale. Chair rules next-rev: a design change is genuinely pending (BOM-only value change on R65/R66) but gated on array data that is not in the repo, and raising the setpoint blind above the array's cold/EOL Voc would fold charge current to zero -- worse than today. Ranked above the decoupling batch because it touches the power budget. Effort small (array characterization + one divider recalculation).

**Action:** Do not change resistors for the Rev2 order. Record the flight array's per-face Vmp and Voc (BOL/EOL, hot/cold) in the repo. If Vmp >= ~12 V: retarget the top leg (keep R69 = 94k; R65 = 220k with R66 = 100k gives 12.0 V; ~394k total gives 14 V) -- BOM-only, no layout impact, applicable to Rev2 boards by rework or to Rev3. If Vmp < ~11.7 V: the LT3652 start-up requirement (not this divider) is the constraint and the charger topology needs a Rev3 review. Either way document the present 'no input regulation' behaviour and measured harvest in the power budget.

**Verify:** Bench with a solar-array simulator or current-limited supply at the measured Vmp/Isc: sweep source current limit below ICHG demand and log VIN and charge current (INA219 U8). Before fix: VIN collapses/hiccups at the current limit; after fix: VIN holds at the setpoint and ICHG folds back smoothly. Add as an ATP pass/fail at a fixed low-illumination point once the divider is retargeted.

## 5. [minor / next-rev / small] E28-2G4M27S (U30) VCC has no local ceramic bypass despite Ebyte's pin-table recommendation and 580 mA TX bursts; nearest +3V3 capacitor 24.8 mm away

**Where:** Root sheet, U30 pin 1 (VCC, +3V3). PCB F.Cu pad 1 at (171.41, 88.90); three +3V3 vias at 1.09/1.18/1.27 mm into the In2.Cu +3V3 pour; nearest +3V3 caps C39 24.81 mm, C18 25.65 mm.

**Why:** Packet id 7, confirmed; verifier downgraded major -> minor because the supply path is the In2 plane via three vias within 1.3 mm (not 25 mm of trace), the rail carries ~69 uF bulk plus ~24 distributed 100 nF, and the module has on-board decoupling. Still a clear deviation from the manufacturer's explicit 'recommend to add external ceramic filter capacitor' on the largest transient load sharing the rail with the RP2350, flash, restored PSRAM, RTC and IMUs. No local cap on V5d (flight, nearest 11.7 mm) or Rev1 either, so three generations have transmitted at 27 dBm without a reported brownout. Ordering within the Rev3 decoupling batch differed (PCB 4, QA 6); chair places it first in the batch because it is the largest transient on the rail that feeds the MCU and both memories and it directly bears on the PSRAM-at-temperature test scope (item 12). Not folded into Rev2: unvalidated edit in the RF area at order time.

**Action:** Rev3: 100 nF 0402 + 10 uF 0603/0805 X5R/X7R directly across U30 pins 1 (VCC) / 2 (GND) within ~2 mm on F.Cu, GND via adjacent to the module GND pad; optionally a small series ferrite into a local 10 uF reservoir to keep the TX transient off the MCU/memory side.

**Verify:** On the Rev2 article: scope +3V3 at U30 pin 1 and at U18 IOVDD during 27 dBm key-up at max duty (targets: droop < ~100 mV at the module, < 50 mV at the MCU). Add a TX-under-load brownout check to ATP: continuous TX bursts while running the PSRAM/flash memtest, zero resets/faults. If droop exceeds ~150 mV, promote to fix-this-rev for Rev3.

## 6. [minor / next-rev / small] TPS54226 (U10) missing TI's recommended 0.1 uF on VCC pin 14 (over-current-limit stability); TPS2HB50 (U25) VBB has no local CVBB; no <=1 uF ceramic anywhere on VBUSP

**Where:** eps_side.kicad_sch: U10 TPS54226 pins 13 VIN / 14 VCC on VBUSP; PCB B.Cu U10 at (186.231,117.650), pad 14 at (183.369,115.700), pad 13 at (183.369,116.350). **Why:** Merges packet id 5 (confirmed minor) with the surviving residual of id 16 (confirmed info; its '8 mm input loop / no ceramic' headline was refuted -- all four VBUSP caps are ceramic MLCCs and C19 effectively abuts U10 VIN/EP). Each is a single 0402 in free B.Cu area, no power-path re-routing.

**Verify:** Bench: step-load and hard-short the 3V3 output with a scope on U10 SW and VIN; confirm clean cycle-by-cycle current limiting with no chatter or erratic trip; capture VIN/VCC ripple during coincident RP2350 + E28 TX bursts. Repeat the short-circuit test after the Rev3 change.

## 7. [minor / next-rev / trivial] TLV1704 watchdog comparator (U1) V+ has no bypass capacitor; nearest VBUSP capacitor 26 mm away (TI SBOS589D Sections 10 / 11.1)

**Where:** watchdog.kicad_sch: U1 pin 3 (V+) on VBUSP, pin 12 (V-) GND. PCB F.Cu U1 at (161.3375,130.300,180), pad 3 at (164.200,130.950), pad 12 at (158.475,130.950); nearest VBUSP cap C25 at 26.04 mm. C20-C24 are all signal/timing caps.

**Why:** Packet id 9, confirmed verbatim by both. TI SBOS589D Section 10 asks for 0.1 uF at the supply pins and Section 11.1 ('Comparators are very sensitive to input noise ... place a decoupling capacitor (0.1-uF ceramic) as close as possible to VS') is not met; the block gates the 3V3 buck enable from very high-impedance nodes (R35 4.7 M timing, 100k hysteresis), so supply noise is a credible source of spurious edges. Inherited unchanged from V5d and Rev1 with no false trips reported through bench/environmental test, so a recommendation-level deviation rather than a defect. Both principals: next-rev; chair concurs and places it third in the batch (zero observed trips across the environmental campaign).

**Action:** Rev3: 100 nF 0402 X7R (>=16 V) directly between U1 pins 3 and 12 within ~2 mm, GND return run under the device per TI Figure 22; optional 1 uF alongside as cheap insurance on a battery-bus-fed comparator.

**Verify:** Scope U1 pin 3 relative to pin 12 during load-switch and radio transients (target < 50 mVpp). Covered functionally by the JP6 ATP (item 2): with the watchdog armed, run >= 24 h (or >= 10 watchdog periods under thermal cycling) with radio TX bursts and load-switch cycling and confirm zero spurious +3V3 resets.

## 8. [minor / next-rev / small] Restored PSRAM (U5) local decoupling is the optional 100 nF (C10) rather than the recommended 1 uF; VDD/VSS stubs 6 mil vs >20 mil suggestion (V5d-identical geometry, but not on the Rev1 environmental article)

**Where:** U5 APS1604M-3SQR-SN B.Cu at (206.900,76.100,180); VDD pad 8 (208.810,73.330), VSS pad 4 (205.000,78.870). C10 100 nF at 1.49 mm; nearest >=1 uF: C43 10 uF 10.57 mm / C65 4.7 uF 10.59 mm. **Why:** Packet id 14, confirmed by both; the separate 'no decoupling at all' claim was refuted (C10 is the dedicated local cap, added in the restore commit). **Verify:** Rev2 ATP: PSRAM full-array pattern + march test at the maximum QSPI clock the firmware uses, with flash XIP active on the shared bus, repeated hot and cold during environmental test, with concurrent 2.4 GHz TX (covers item 5); scope U5 VDD at pin 8 during burst reads (target droop < 100 mV). ## 11. [minor / accept-with-rationale / trivial] All load-switch / high-side-switch fault and status outputs unconnected and diagnostics disabled: no overcurrent, charger-fault or deployment-channel telemetry (observability gap, not a manufacturer deviation)

**Where:** load_switches.kicad_sch: U19/U21/U22/U23/U24/U27/U29 AP22653 ~{Fault} pin 4 (x7, single-node nets); U6 TPS4H160A-Q1 ~{ST1..4} pins 7-10 unconnected, DIAG_EN pin 14 via R101 100k to GND; U25 TPS2HB50A-Q1 DIA_EN pin 16 via R55 15k to GND, SNS pin 2 into R81 1k to GND (no ADC); eps_side: IC6 LT3652 ~{FAULT} pin 5 unconnected. U9 MCP23017 has exactly one spare GPIO (GPB6, item 3).

**Why:** Packet id 21, confirmed on the facts by both, but not a manufacturer deviation: TI's TPS2HB50-Q1 unused-pin table explicitly permits DIA_EN grounded through RPROT, TPS4H160 DIAG_EN has an internal pulldown and ST1-4 are open-drain (safe floating), AP22653 FLAG is open-drain. Current limiting itself is fully configured (47k ILIM x7 ~600 mA, U6 CL via R102, U25 ILIM via R83/R84), so protection is intact. The proposed MCP23017 remedy is not implementable (GPA0/GPA4/GPA7 are ENABLE_Heater/RF2_RX_EN/RF2_TX_EN; INTA/INTB are outputs), so any hardware fix needs RP2350 GPIO or another expander -- a Rev3 scope decision. ## Refuted
- **USB-C connector CC1/CC2 pins are fully unterminated (no Rd pull-down), inherited from Rev1** — The finding's single piece of evidence - that Net-(J12-CC1) and Net-(J12-CC2) each contain only the connector pin - is not reproducible. Both nets carry a 5.1k 0603 resistor to GND: R26 on CC1 and R27 on CC2. These are exactly the Rd sink terminations the USB Type-C spec requires of a UFP so that a C-to-C source detects the sink and turns on VBUS, and 5.1k is the nominal value. The reviewer claims to have checked both the Rev2 netlist and a fresh Rev1 export and to have found single-node nets in both; I ran the same query on the same two files and got the resistors in both. The concern is therefore fully addressed by the existing design and there is nothing to accept or fix. Downgraded to info because there is no defect at all - not a minor one.
- **Restored APS1604M PSRAM (U5) has no local decoupling capacitor - datasheet calls a 1 uF low-ESR ceramic on VDD a requirement** — The finding's headline and four of its five load-bearing evidence claims do not reproduce. U5 does have a dedicated local decoupling capacitor: C10, a 100 nF 0402 on the same layer, 1.49 mm from the VDD pad, directly routed, and added in the very same commit as the PSRAM with the commit message naming it as the decoupling. The assertions that no capacitor was added, that none is assigned to U5, that C10 is a shared QSPI-region bypass the flash also depends on, that C69 is the nearest +3V3 bulk, and that the placement is new Rev2 work are each contradicted by the netlist diff, the PCB pad coordinates, the net assignments and the V5d baseline. The reviewer appears to have reached the wrong conclusion by scanning schematic-sheet XY proximity — where C10 sits 31.9 mm from U5 because schematic placement is arbitrary — instead of the netlist connection and PCB geometry that actually determine decoupling. What genuinely survives is a much narrower and materially smaller point: the local cap is 100 nF where the datasheet recommends a low-ESR 1 uF and calls the 0.1 uF the optional high-frequency adder, the VDD/VSS traces are 6 mil against a suggested >20 mil, and VSS has a single ground via rather than multipoint. That residual is real and worth a cheap fix, but it is 'the local cap is the wrong value' rather than 'there is no local cap' — a different and far less alarming claim than the one filed, and it carries no spaceflight-specific risk framing, since this exact arrangement (same parts, same coordinates) is what flew on V5d_Production. Because the finding as written asserts an absence that demonstrably is not there, the honest verdict is refuted; I set severity to minor to carry the residual value/trace-width deviation forward rather than lose it. One genuinely actionable item did surface in this area while checking, though it is outside this finding's scope: drc.json contains four real clearance ERRORs at C10, the worst being 0.0892 mm actual against a 0.2000 mm rule at pad 1 (+3V3) at (210.15, 74.00), all new in Rev2 with the restored PSRAM — that is a manufacturability issue worth fixing before the JLCPCB order.
- **LIS2MDL (U15) C1 pin capacitor is 220 nF where the datasheet specifies 100 nF** — The finding inverts the datasheet. ST specifies C1 = 220 nF for the LIS2MDL, stated twice in tabular form (Table 2 pin description, p. 8/41; Table 14 internal pin status, p. 20/41) and again in Figure 5, the application circuit. The design fits C31 = 220 nF, which is exactly the manufacturer-specified value — there is no deviation at all, let alone a 2.2x over-value. The reviewer's error is traceable: in section 5 the paragraph about C1 sits immediately below the sentence 'Power supply decoupling capacitors (100 nF ceramic, 10 uF aluminum) should be placed as near as possible to pin 9 of the device', and the 100 nF from that supply-decoupling sentence was misattributed to the C1 pin capacitor. Notably the design already satisfies that separate 100 nF / 10 uF requirement independently via C34 (100 nF 0402) and C43 (10 uF 0603) on Vdd pin 9, which the reviewer themselves observed without noticing it accounted for the 100 nF they were looking for. I also could not reproduce the reviewer's placement number: recomputing the footprint rotation from the PCB gives 0.988 mm pad-to-pad from C31 pad 1 to U15 pad 5 (and 1.093 mm from C31 pad 2 to U15 pad 6 GND), not 1.77 mm. That does not change the outcome — the reviewer and I agree placement is fine — but it is a second unreproduced number in the same item. The only genuine C1 requirement that could be checked and was not is the 'highly recommended low ESR (max 200 mOhm)' constraint; a 220 nF 0402 MLCC is typically well under 50 mOhm ESR, so that is satisfied by construction and is not a finding either. No respin action is warranted; acting on the recommendation as written would substitute a wrong value for the correct one and degrade the set/reset pulse reservoir. Inherited identically from Rev1 and flight-heritage V5d, consistent with it being correct rather than a latent defect. Severity set to info because there is no defect to rate.
